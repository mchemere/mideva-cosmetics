Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is delivery within Nairobi and upcountry Kenya?

Orders placed before 2:00 PM in Nairobi are dispatched the same day, with delivery typically taking 2 to 4 hours. Deliveries outside Nairobi (Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret) arrive via expedited courier within 24 to 48 hours.

Can I pay using Lipa na M-Pesa?

Yes! We accept direct Lipa na M-Pesa payments, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, and Cash on Delivery for addresses within the greater Nairobi metropolitan area.

Are Mideva Cosmetics formulas suitable for sensitive skin?

Absolutely. All our products are dermatologically tested, non-comedogenic, and 100% free from harsh sulfates, parabens, and irritating synthetic dyes. We always recommend patch testing new products on your inner forearm 24 hours prior to regular use.

What is your return and exchange policy?

We offer a hassle-free 7-day return policy for any unopened, factory-sealed products in their original packaging. For hygiene reasons, cosmetics and skincare products that have been unsealed or used cannot be returned.
