Our Origin & Vision

Celebrating Natural Beauty with Uncompromising Luxury

Mideva Cosmetics was established with a singular mission: to provide sophisticated, safe, and profoundly efficacious cosmetics and skincare designed specifically to complement personal styles and illuminate every skin tone.

For decades, beauty enthusiasts in East Africa faced a dilemma: choose between harsh, damaging skin brighteners or imported luxury lines formulated without rich melanin undertones in mind. Mideva bridges this gap by harmonizing nutrient-dense East African superfoods with cutting-edge clean cosmetic science.

"True beauty is not about covering who you are; it is about nourishing your skin so your authentic inner glow radiates effortlessly."

Ethical Sourcing

The African Botanical Advantage

We sustainably partner with local women cooperatives across Kenya and East Africa to harvest pure, cold-pressed botanicals that thrive in equatorial soils.

🌳

Wild Baobab Seed

Known as the African Tree of Life. Packed with 6x more Vitamin C than oranges and dense Omega fatty acids to plump and firm cellular structure.

🌰

Nilotica Shea Butter

A rare, silky variety of East African shea that melts immediately at skin temperature, delivering 48 hours of deep barrier restoration.

☕

Highland Kenyan Coffee

Grown in volcanic slopes around Mt. Kenya, rich in organic caffeine and polyphenols that naturally tone, depuff, and awaken fatigued skin.
