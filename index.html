The Mideva Story

Where Pure Botanicals Meet Modern Science

Born in Nairobi, Mideva Cosmetics was founded with a singular, empowering vision: to celebrate diverse skin tones with clean, luxurious beauty essentials that truly nourish from within.

We believe that authentic beauty begins with self-love and uncompromising quality. That is why every drop of our serums, every sweep of our lip nectars, and every mist of our perfumes combines nutrient-rich African botanicals like Marula oil, Nilotica Shea, and Baobab with gold-standard dermatological actives.
